3. The known artefact in the shipped candidate

The candidate's emitted pattern alternates between row pairs: even rows carry 0.00 times as many emitted pixels as odd rows, and the row-density power peaks at periods of 2 and 4 rows -- exactly the GeoDAWN flight-line spacings (200 m in Area 1, 400 m in Area 2). The cause is measured: the airborne grids carry a ~2.5% row-pair correlation (magnetic channels included) and the isotropic 3x3 thinning amplifies it. A de-striped variant (3-tap [1,2,1] smoothing along rows before thinning) was built and put through the same frozen holdout; it wins 2 of 4 rules, so the pre-registered rule keeps the striped file. Both numbers are published rather than hidden.
